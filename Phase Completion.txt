----------------------------------------------

- Starts empty without writing when no save exists.

30. ARTWORK TRANSACTION AND RECOVERY ORDER
------------------------------------------

Artwork replacement now follows this verified sequence:

1. Confirm that the file is a non-empty image.
2. Decode the image and verify usable dimensions.
3. Generate a fresh Nightforge artwork key.
4. Store the blob under the staged key in IndexedDB.
5. Read the staged blob back and verify that it exists.
6. Save the Scene envelope with the new artwork reference.
7. Add the previous key to the durable pending-deletion set as part of that
   same Scene save.
8. Delete the prior artwork blob only after the new Scene reference is saved.
9. Remove the pending key only after blob deletion is confirmed.

Failure behavior:

- Decode failure leaves the prior Scene and artwork untouched.
- Staged-write failure leaves the prior Scene and artwork untouched.
- Staged-read failure deletes the staged orphan and keeps prior valid data.
- Missing staged readback is treated as verification failure.
- Scene-envelope save failure deletes the staged orphan and retains the prior
  Scene reference.
- Old-artwork deletion failure keeps the new valid Scene reference and a
  durable pending cleanup key.
- Cleanup-acknowledgement failure leaves the pending key retryable even when
  the blob has already been removed.
- Startup retries every pending deletion.
- Startup also reconciles and removes unreferenced staged orphan blobs.
- A failed cleanup never restores an old Scene reference or resurrects a
  deleted Scene.

White Canvas uses the same persistence-first safety model:

1. Save artworkKey as null and blankCanvas as true.
2. Durably schedule the previous key for deletion.
3. Delete the blob.
4. Acknowledge cleanup only after confirmed deletion.

104. ATTACK SELECTION AND TARGETING
-----------------------------------

The Action drawer now exposes only weapons actually equipped in a token's main
or off hand. Every option retains its stable item and hand identity. Attack
availability reports exact reasons for missing equipment, spent Action,
incapacitation, defeat, or an incompatible Swap branch.

Selecting a weapon enters an explicit targeting mode:

- Legal enemy tokens receive semantic target controls and a Nightforge-native
  target state.
- Escape, the targeting legend, or the existing veil cancels without spending
  a resource.
- Out-of-range and full-wall-blocked targets cannot resolve and spend nothing.
- Input is locked during a resolving cinematic so one resource cannot be spent
  twice.
- A successful main-hand attack spends Action only. It never spends movement,
  never spends Bonus, and never advances initiative.
- Normal Attack permits any remaining movement. Attack after Swap preserves the
  Phase 8 rule that later movement is locked.
- Attack blocks later Dash and Swap as required by the active-turn branch.


105. RANGE AND LINE OF SIGHT
----------------------------

The attack overlay is one bounded SVG aligned to the Table grid. It produces
one stair-stepped region path per active range tier rather than one rendered
node per cell.

- Melee and Reach use the green tier.
- Ranged normal range is green and long range is yellow.
- Thrown melee distance is green, normal thrown range is yellow, and long
  thrown range is red.
- Diagonal attack distance follows the existing Battle grid convention: one
  diagonal cell costs five feet.
- Full walls block ranged and thrown line of sight.
- Half walls permit ranged and thrown attacks with disadvantage.
- Melee attacks ignore walls for line-of-sight purposes.
- Hidden walls retain their mechanical effect without becoming visible.
- Out-of-range or blocked clicks never mutate the Scene or spend Action.


106. ATTACK ROLLS AND DAMAGE
----------------------------

The attack engine uses an injectable random source and returns explicit Scene
patches plus a presentation outcome. It implements:

- One d20 for normal, two keep-high for advantage, and two keep-low for
  disadvantage.
- Cancellation when at least one advantage and one disadvantage source oppose
  one another.
- Strength for melee, Dexterity for ranged, and the better modifier for
  Finesse; thrown melee weapons retain their normal melee ability rule.
- Proficiency and the equipped weapon's magic bonus.
- Natural 1 as an automatic miss and natural 20 as an automatic hit and
  critical.
- Critical damage that doubles dice only, never fixed damage or modifiers.
- Ability damage applied once and a final damage floor of zero.
- Long range, long thrown range, Small creatures using Heavy weapons, a Lance
  at five feet, Attack after Swap, half-wall cover, and applicable conditions
  as named roll-mode sources.

Resolved hit-point changes, spent resources, attack branch state, and the
dual-wield follow-up are persisted together as one Scene update.


107. DUAL WIELDING AND BONUS ATTACK
-----------------------------------

A main Action attack unlocks exactly one off-hand follow-up only when both
equipped weapons are Light melee weapons and neither violates two-hand or
shield legality. The unlocked weapon is always the other equipped hand.

- A Swap-branch attack never unlocks the off-hand follow-up.
- The Bonus drawer reports the exact legal weapon and unavailable reason.
- The off-hand attack uses the normal attack ability, proficiency, and magic
  calculations.
- A positive ability modifier is omitted from off-hand damage.
- A negative ability modifier is retained in off-hand damage.
- The Bonus attack spends Bonus only and never ends the turn.
- Chest interaction and physical weapon retrieval remain visibly deferred to
  Phase 10.


108. CONDITIONS
---------------

Nightforge now has one stable catalog containing exactly these fifteen manual
conditions: Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Grappled, Incapacitated,
Invisible, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Stunned,
Unconscious, and Exhaustion.

The selected-token inspector provides reversible manual toggles and explanatory
notes without changing the established inspector layout. Active conditions are
persisted on the Battle token and displayed as compact badges on the Table.

Structured effects cover attacker advantage/disadvantage, incoming melee and
ranged advantage/disadvantage, immobility, incapacitation, and automatic melee
critical hits against Paralyzed or Unconscious targets. Opposing sources cancel
normally. Charmed, Deafened, and level-unspecified Exhaustion remain tracked
manual states without inventing unsupported automation. Conditions are never
automatically inflicted by attacks.


109. ATTACK CINEMATICS AND FEEDBACK
-----------------------------------

The attack presentation is a staged Nightforge-native overlay with explicit
spin, natural-roll, modifier, verdict, damage, and impact states.

- Advantage and disadvantage show both dice and de-emphasize the rejected die.
- The selected natural result, ability/proficiency/magic modifier total, target
  AC, hit/miss/critical verdict, damage dice, damage modifier, and HP impact are
  all exposed.
- Hits receive a brief target impact state and floating damage feedback.
- One newly authored procedural impact sound plays on a successful hit only;
  misses are silent and no external media asset was copied.
- Reduced-motion preference shortens or suppresses nonessential movement while
  retaining the complete result information.
- Persistence failure enters a visible failed stage and does not apply the
  speculative attack state.


110. PERSISTENCE AND FAILURE SAFETY
-----------------------------------

Attack and condition commands use the existing Nightforge Scene repository
boundary. The domain layer creates deterministic patches and the interface
commits them only through the application command surface.

- Attack results, hit points, resources, branch state, off-hand unlocks, and
  conditions survive repository reconstruction.
- Invalid targets and unavailable commands are pure refusals.
- A failed attack write preserves the entire last valid Battle.
- Cinematic state, range geometry, target hover/selection, impact animation,
  floating damage, and procedural audio are transient.
- No original Roll30 key, path, runtime, or user record is read or written.
